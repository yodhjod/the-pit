## Round 2: approved so far (this section overrides the tables below)

**Layers (final):**
- **L1 Bastione + Marina.** Saint Remy terrace and stairs, Marina's narrow alleys, trattorie (La Pola), tourists.
- **L2 Sunken Via Roma.** The porticoes half under water, metro works, transenne, CTM buses drowned.
- **L3 Tuvixeddu.** Rock-cut Punic tombs.
- **L4 Rotatorie.** A spiral of roundabouts going down (Zedda's plan: new roundabouts in Piazza d'Armi, by the port, two around Piazza Matteotti, Sant'Avendrace-style for Via Roma). Signs reading ROTATORIA, flowerbeds in the middle, cars circling forever.
- **L5 Sant'Elia.** The neighbourhood at the bottom: the *palazzoni*, the old abandoned Sant'Elia stadium, the Lazzaretto, the Unipol Domus next door.

**Enemies:** seagulls, flamingoes, young *maranza* (tracksuit, man-bag, scooter), ultras in Cagliari Calcio shirts, plus the E-table moves.
**Brands:** all approved, plus **Nonna Isa** supermarket and **pizzette sarde** (pastry tray as collectibles or as a vendor item).
**Politicians:** P1 Todde, P2 Zedda (now also: the roundabout man), P3 Truzzu, P4 Solinas, P5 Soru, P8 Giulini. **Salvini and Meloni out.**
**Culture:** Sant'Efisio procession (M7), CTM, Abbanoa.

**Two people, both in kind roles:**
- **Checco ("al burro").** A friendly NPC, not a joke at his expense. Proposal: he's the cheerful guide who shows up at each vendor and gives a tip about the next level, and "AL BURRO!" is the Perfect-hit callout and the level-clear cheer. Kekkeddu = Checco? (see questions)
- **Antonello Lai, "Tziu Lai"** (Sant'Elia journalist, died Aug 2024; the whole neighbourhood came to his funeral). He created *Zona Franca* on TCS, airtime for people nobody else listened to. Proposal: in L5 Sant'Elia he's the voice of the lore, a reporter with a TCS microphone interviewing the pit's forgotten people. Respectful, no jokes about him.

**Slang:** "Cancaru!" is a Sardinian curse, from Latin *cancer*, roughly "damn it!" / "accidenti!". If your group doesn't say it, it's out. Tell me what you *do* say when something goes wrong and I'll use that.

**Round 3 answers:**
- **"I gabbiani con il becco woo woo"**: a Cagliari seagull meme. A viral clip (a Sardinia Post piece credits "Sensational Gianni"), now with TikTok #beccowoowoo, a remix song ("I GABBIANI WOO WOO") and an Instagram fan page. Use: the seagull enemies are **becco-woo-woo gulls**, they cry "WOO WOO!" as they swoop, and the L1 miniboss is a giant woo-woo seagull. If you know the exact original line, send it and I'll quote it.
- **Kekkeddu = Checco.** He's the friendly guide (above). Still need: **the voicelines you want, written out the way he says them.**
- **La Pola**: dropped.
- **Maranza** (Treccani neologism 2025: noisy youth groups, tracksuits, man-bag, branded sneakers). In-game name: **gaggi / burdi** (your local words). Enemy in L2 and L5: a gaggio on a scooter, man-bag, Cagliari Calcio shirt. Note: the word "maranza" is also used with a racist edge against North African kids, so I'll draw them as generic Cagliari gaggi and keep them about the style, not ethnicity.

## L: Layers (the five stretches of the pit)

The pit opens in the middle of Cagliari. Each layer is a slice of the city's underside, going down.

| ID | Layer | Look | Ledge / obstacle |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | **Castello / Bastione di Saint Remy** (replaces Rim) | White limestone terrace and the big arched staircase, Torre dell'Elefante and San Pancrazio towers on the skyline, tourist stalls on the rim. Sunset orange. | Bastione stairs; café tables, selfie sticks, cruise tourists as crowd |
| L2 | **Via Roma / the metro works** (replaces Drowned Metro) | Via Roma porticoes, the port behind, orange-and-white *transenne* everywhere, the half-built metro line, fallen overhead cables. | Ledge of road plates and site fencing; obstacle = construction barrier |
| L3 | **Cagliari sotterranea** (replaces Ossuary) | Tuvixeddu rock-cut tombs, Roman amphitheatre steps, Santa Restituta crypt, WWII air-raid shelters (the city really did hide in its caves in 1943). | Rock-cut stairs; bones swapped for amphorae, Punic tomb shafts |
| L4 | **Molentargius / Poetto** (replaces Spore Gardens) | Pink salt pans, flamingoes, the Poetto beach with its *dark* sand (the 2002 beach-nourishment fiasco), Sella del Diavolo on the horizon, kiosks. | Ledge of salt-pan dykes and beach boardwalk |
| L5 | **The Depths: Abbanoa's pipes** (replaces Depths) | Below the city: leaking water mains, sewer tunnels, rusted valves, Saras-refinery glow from somewhere off-screen. Dark teal and black. | Ledge of giant pipes; obstacle = burst main |

## E: Enemies and obstacles

Each one maps to an existing move. The move itself doesn't change.

| ID | Thing | Move | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Seagull (gabbiano reale)** stealing your panino | swooper (swipe down = duck) | Big yellow beak with the red spot, grey back. Very readable. |
| E2 | **Flamingo** charging on stilt legs | critter (tap = whack) | Pink S-neck, bent black beak. The single most recognisable Cagliari animal. |
| E3 | **Rat from Via Roma** | critter, Via Roma layer | Simple silhouette, adds variety |
| E4 | **Mosquito swarm from Molentargius** | swooper, L4 | Cloud of pixels; may read as noise at small size |
| E5 | **Transenna barricade** (orange-white) | kick barricade (swipe right) | The "città delle transenne" (Zedda's own words) |
| E6 | **CRAI shopping trolley** | two-thumb cart shove | Red CRAI logo on the side |
| E7 | **Falling chunk of balcony/cornice** | brace (rockfall) | Cagliari has real stories of debris falling from old buildings onto cars |
| E8 | **Open manhole / Abbanoa hole in the road** | missing step (jump) | With a little Abbanoa sign |
| E9 | **Fallen metro overhead cable** | zipline (hold) | Real incident: tram cables fell on cars in Via Roma |
| E10 | **Bag of abandoned rubbish** | jump obstacle | Pirri/Is Mirrionis-style dumped bags; seagulls on top |
